What we teach

The Martial Arts Do Bu Teaches

Led by experienced Teachers.

Judo

A throwing and grappling art focused on using leverage and technique over strength, developing balance, control and discipline both on and off the mat.

Jiu-Jitsu

Ground based grappling and submissions, ideal for building real self-defence skill regardless of size or strength, with a strong focus on technique and problem solving.

Karate

A striking art built on structured forms (kata), sparring and discipline, suited to students who enjoy a strong sense of technique, tradition and progression through belts.

Muay Thai

Known as the "art of eight limbs," combining punches, kicks, elbows and knees for a high-intensity striking discipline and serious conditioning workout.
